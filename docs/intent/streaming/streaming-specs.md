# Streaming surfaces specs

Design: [streaming-design.md](streaming-design.md). Prefix `ING`, facets GRPC, NIMWS, EOU.

## Riva gRPC servicer (GRPC)

- [ ] **ING-GRPC-001**: The servicer shall implement all three `RivaSpeechRecognition` RPCs (`StreamingRecognize`, `Recognize`, `GetRivaSpeechRecognitionConfig`) with message types from the public `nvidia-riva-client` package's generated stubs.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-002**: In `StreamingRecognize`, the first request message shall be `streaming_config` (otherwise ING-LIFE-001), the handler shall complete validation and `open` before advancing the request iterator, every later request message shall carry `audio_content`, and a later `streaming_config` shall fail with `protocol_order` and abnormal lease cleanup; the session shall open with `cadence="560ms"` and the validated locale (ING-CORE-015), cadence being server policy because the Riva protocol has no cadence field; each changed cumulative hypothesis returned by `SessionLease.feed` shall project to a `StreamingRecognizeResponse` with `is_final=false` when `interim_results` is true; and client half-close shall map to `flush`, producing exactly one `is_final=true` response after finish and release and before the server closes the stream.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-026**: Each `StreamingRecognizeResponse` shall carry one result holding one alternative whose `transcript` is the cumulative hypothesis, and the frontend shall set no other result field than `is_final` (ING-GRPC-002), so no word offsets, stability or channel tag.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-027**: The servicer shall relay the model's transcript text unchanged.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-028**: When a shutdown finalization succeeds, the servicer shall send exactly one `is_final=true` result and then end the stream as ING-ERR-027 defines.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-015**: When RPC cancellation, a deadline, or an abort during shutdown wins before final delivery, the servicer shall emit no fabricated final response, a cleanup failure shall not replace the already-selected cancellation or deadline status, and a cleanup failure on an otherwise normal path shall follow ING-LIFE-020.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-005**: Every `RecognitionConfig` field shall be dispositioned by the field disposition table of the streaming design as honoured, model-intrinsic or rejected-when-non-default, rejected fields shall answer `INVALID_ARGUMENT` (or `UNIMPLEMENTED` for absent capability classes) naming the field, and no field shall be silently ignored.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-025**: A contract test shall walk `RecognitionConfig.DESCRIPTOR` of the pinned `nvidia-riva-client` stubs and fail when a field lacks a disposition.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-006**: The servicer shall accept `enable_automatic_punctuation` and `verbatim_transcripts` under either value without altering output, and shall state the deviation in its user-facing documentation and in the configuration RPC's description.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-022**: The servicer shall reject `profanity_filter`, `enable_word_time_offsets` and `enable_separate_recognition_per_channel` when true as `invalid_config_field` naming the field, with status `INVALID_ARGUMENT`.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-023**: The servicer shall reject a non-empty `speech_contexts`, a `diarization_config` with `enable_speaker_diarization` true or any field set, and an `endpointing_config` with any non-zero field as `unsupported_capability` with status `UNIMPLEMENTED` naming the field, and shall reject a non-empty `custom_configuration` as `invalid_config_field`.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-024**: The servicer shall accept `model` empty or equal to the served-model identity and reject any other value naming `model`, and shall accept for `language_code` any key of the factory's `locales`, taking `default_locale` when it is empty.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-017**: The servicer shall treat a zero `audio_channel_count` and a zero `max_alternatives` as unset and normalize each to 1, and shall reject a larger `max_alternatives` and a larger `audio_channel_count` naming the field.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-018**: Under a concrete encoding the servicer shall validate `sample_rate_hertz` and `audio_channel_count` as the concrete audio configuration against the accept-matrix and answer `unsupported_format` naming `sample_rate_hertz` for a zero rate, and under `ENCODING_UNSPECIFIED` it shall treat a zero value of either field as unconstrained and a non-zero value as a constraint the resolved header must match.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-016**: When a streaming request carries a non-empty request-level `runtime_config` rider, the servicer shall reject it as `invalid_config_field` naming `runtime_config` before `SessionFactory.open`, and shall not treat it as a `RecognitionConfig` field or silently ignore it.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-029**: When `RecognitionConfig.encoding` is `ENCODING_UNSPECIFIED`, the servicer shall resolve encoding, sample rate and channel count from a RIFF/WAVE header at the head of the audio, strip the header bytes, validate the resolved cell against the accept-matrix, treat declared `sample_rate_hertz` or `audio_channel_count` as constraints the header must match, run streaming RPCs as a fully validated deferred-header mode (complete `open` before reading audio, buffer at most `max_riff_header_bytes` after `open`, call no `feed` until resolution), have unary `Recognize` resolve and validate its complete request before taking a file-gate permit, answer `unsupported_format` naming the fields when no complete parseable header is found within the bound or the cell is off-matrix, and never override a declared non-`UNSPECIFIED` encoding with a header.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-021**: When resolving a RIFF/WAVE header, the servicer shall treat a `data` chunk length of 0 or `0xFFFFFFFF` as audio to the end of the stream, treat any other length as explicit and truncate the audio at that many bytes, and ignore chunks after `data`.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-019**: When a legal `StreamingRecognize` message carries more decoded audio than `pre_submit_max_samples`, the handler shall split it into `feed` calls of at most `pre_submit_max_samples` samples and pause reading from the transport while it feeds.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-020**: The pre-submit layer shall not project `buffer_overflow` for a legal message on a streaming surface, and the code shall remain reserved at that layer.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-030**: At `init_state`, `riva_frontend` shall require finite positive `unary_max_encoded_audio_bytes`, `unary_max_decoded_duration_seconds`, `max_riff_header_bytes` and `grpc_config_envelope_max_bytes`, `grpc_receive_max_bytes` shall equal the encoded-audio limit plus the envelope allowance so that every handler-reachable audio or duration overflow returns `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` with code `request_too_large` before the server opens an engine session or takes a file-gate permit (a message above the framework cap may receive gRPC's native `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` framing error and is the sole exception to the stable catalog-detail rule), and before allocating decoded or resampled audio the handler shall derive duration from bounded payload bytes and the fixed-width mono cell (PCM16 two bytes per sample, G.711 one byte per sample).
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-010**: Unary `Recognize` request handling shall process request audio in bounded memory and shall perform no filesystem write, local spool or retained upload-artifact creation on success, rejection, cancellation, deadline or failure; client fixture reads and test-result writes are outside this prohibition.
- [ ] **ING-GRPC-014**: `GetRivaSpeechRecognitionConfig` shall return a static description listing the resolved served-model identity with `parameters["type"]` equal to `streaming`, and also with `type` equal to `offline` when the shared files-enabled flag is true, each entry carrying sample rates `8000,16000`, encodings `LINEAR_PCM,MULAW,ALAW`, the factory's `locales`, and the model-intrinsic notes for punctuation and verbatim transcripts.

## Riva Realtime WebSocket (NIMWS)

- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-011**: A pure-ASGI dispatcher added as middleware by `attach_router` shall claim a connection only when the scope type is `websocket`, the path (after `root_path`) is exactly `/v1/realtime`, and strict percent and UTF-8 decoding yields the query-pair multiset exactly `{("intent", "transcription")}`, and every other scope, including a missing, blank, duplicate, unsupported, additional or malformed query, shall pass unchanged to core.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-019**: The dispatcher shall not delete, reorder, replace or interpret core's routes or events.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-001**: The adapter shall serve `POST /v1/realtime/transcription_sessions` on the existing HTTP listener and return the default transcription-session object shaped per the public NIM Realtime reference, advertising the resolved served-model identity.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-013**: The bootstrap response shall set `input_audio_transcription.language` to the factory's `default_locale` (empty when it is `None`), and a Realtime session that names no locale shall use `default_locale`, requiring the client to name one when it is `None`.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-020**: The bootstrap default object shall carry `modalities` equal to `["text"]`, `input_audio_format` equal to `pcm16`, `input_audio_params` of 16000 Hz and 1 channel, `recognition_config` with `max_alternatives` 1, disabled `speaker_diarization` and `word_boosting`, an empty `endpointing_config`, and `client_secret` null.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-021**: On an admitted claimed WebSocket connect, the adapter shall send `conversation.created` before processing client events.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-016**: When a claimed connection is refused by the ingress cap or by a draining plugin, the adapter shall accept the WebSocket, send one `error` carrying the catalog code (`busy` with its `authority`, or `service_unavailable`), close with 1013, send no `conversation.created`, and create no owner or lease.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-004**: Realtime client and server events shall map directly to the lease: `transcription_session.update` to validate-and-open or serialized locale update followed by truthful `transcription_session.updated`, `input_audio_buffer.append` to front end plus bounded `lease.feed`, `input_audio_buffer.done` to flush, finish and release, a changed cumulative hypothesis to a true incremental `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.delta`, the final after release to exactly one `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.completed` with `is_last_result: true` followed by close 1000, a transcription failure to `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.failed`, and a protocol or session failure to `error`; malformed JSON, a non-object or malformed envelope, an unknown event type, or structurally invalid required fields or types on a known event shall mutate no state, emit one field-specific `error` with catalog code `invalid_event` when a response can be serialized safely and close 1008, while a structurally valid event carrying unsupported configuration shall take ING-NIMWS-022's specific disposition; traffic racing the terminal completion shall observe closure and cause no second feed, final or state transition; and delta events shall carry only the new text with no interim `completed` events.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-017**: When a shutdown finalization succeeds, the adapter shall send the same single `completed` event with `is_last_result: true` and then end the session as ING-ERR-027 defines.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-005**: The `input_audio_buffer.commit` event shall be advisory, acknowledged with `input_audio_buffer.committed` and never forcing a sub-cadence engine step, and only `input_audio_buffer.done` shall trigger final-tail flush.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-006**: Each Realtime owner shall process accepted commands through one FIFO stream, every accepted `input_audio_buffer.append` ahead of `input_audio_buffer.clear` shall complete its bounded lease handoff before `clear` takes effect, `clear` shall drop only raw-byte residual, deferred-header bytes and decoded pieces still local to the frontend and acknowledge `input_audio_buffer.cleared`, shall never cancel or rewind audio handed to `feed`, audio the lease accepted or model state, and clearing a partial deferred RIFF header shall restart the bounded scan from empty.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-007**: `input_audio_format` and `input_audio_params` shall be validated jointly against the accept-matrix `(pcm16, 16000, mono)`, `(pcm16, 8000, mono)`, `(g711_ulaw, 8000, mono)` and `(g711_alaw, 8000, mono)`, and every other combination, including 16 kHz G.711 or non-mono input, shall answer `unsupported_format` naming the offending fields.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-009**: When `input_audio_format` is `"none"`, the adapter shall treat it as a fully validated deferred-header mode (complete `open` before reading audio, buffer at most `max_riff_header_bytes` after it, resolve and strip a RIFF/WAVE header per ING-GRPC-029, validate the cell against the accept-matrix, enforce declared `input_audio_params` as constraints, call no `feed` until resolution), answer `unsupported_format` naming the fields on failure, never override a declared non-`"none"` format with a header, and not add `"none"` to the honoured OpenAI enum.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-022**: The first session update shall disposition `recognition_config`, `speaker_diarization`, `word_boosting` and `endpointing_config` field for field like the gRPC table, `input_audio_transcription.model` shall be empty or the served-model identity, the default object shall round-trip without rejection while the factory's `default_locale` is set (when it is `None`, the default object's empty language requires the client to name a locale, ING-NIMWS-013), with sections becoming non-default only through their enable toggle, non-zero windows or non-emptiness, a non-empty `input_audio_transcription.prompt` and modalities other than `["text"]` shall be rejected, `id`, `object`, `client_secret` and `provenance` shall be echo keys, unknown keys shall answer `invalid_config_field` naming the dialect-native field, and initial configuration shall be all-or-nothing (every field error emitted, no `open`, no `transcription_session.updated`, and the adapter left unconfigured for a corrected update).
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-014**: The Realtime adapter shall reject a non-empty `custom_configuration` in the session update as `invalid_config_field` naming `custom_configuration`.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-012**: The dispatcher shall enforce `ws_receive_max_bytes` itself by counting the bytes of every message received on a claimed connection, shall close with 1009 a message above it (preceded by an `error` carrying `request_too_large` when the connection can still send), and `init_state` shall fail when `ws_receive_max_bytes` is smaller than `ws_event_envelope_max_bytes`.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-015**: When a received message is above `ws_event_envelope_max_bytes` but within `ws_receive_max_bytes`, the adapter shall, before any JSON or base64 decode, send one `error` carrying `request_too_large` and close with 1009.
- [ ] **ING-NIMWS-018**: Before resample allocation the adapter shall derive the decoded sample count of an `input_audio_buffer.append` and reject one that cannot fit the bounded path, and shall split a legal append into `feed` calls of at most `pre_submit_max_samples` samples while pausing reads.

## End-of-utterance (EOU)

- [D] **ING-EOU-005**: Where the end-of-utterance phase is enabled, utterance segmentation shall implement the public protocol's documented window and threshold semantics (`start_history` and `start_threshold`, `stop_history` and `stop_threshold`, `stop_history_eou` and `stop_threshold_eou`), with the stop-condition interpretation taken from the public Speech NIM documentation, over a per-unit blank and non-blank mask that the model plugin would have to report and does not yet specify.
- [D] **ING-EOU-002**: Where the end-of-utterance phase is enabled, the session shall emit a segment final on an end-of-utterance and request decoder-state reset through a segment-reset interface of the model plugin, an obligation the model plugin does not yet specify.
- [ ] **ING-EOU-003**: Until the end-of-utterance phase lands, a non-default `endpointing_config` shall be rejected per the field disposition tables (ING-GRPC-005), and when it lands the six fields shall become honoured values and this requirement shall be superseded by ING-EOU-005.
- [D] **ING-EOU-006**: Where the end-of-utterance phase is enabled, its behaviour shall be validated by unit tests derived from the documented semantics (synthetic silence and speech patterns with known window outcomes) and, as an advisory lane, by comparison with the behaviour described in the public Speech NIM reference, and never by golden parity, because the reference model has no endpointing.
